How do I play?

Tap a blob. It pops and fires a beam along its arrow, and the first blob the beam hits pops and fires too. Clear the board in par for 3 stars. You always get one spare tap. Press and hold a blob to see where its beam will land.

I bought something and it's missing

Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account. Reinstalling on the same account restores Remove ads and any looks you bought. If something still isn't there, email us with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history.

Ads and privacy

You can change your ad choices in the game under Settings. Read the full privacy policy.
