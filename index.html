Hammerfort Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Hammerfort mobile game ("the Game", "we", "us") collects and why. The Game is published by Bradley Blake (United Kingdom). Contact: games@bradblake.co.uk.

What we collect

DataWhyWho processes it
A random anonymous player IDTo save your progress to the cloud and show leaderboardsGoogle Firebase (Authentication, Firestore)
Your game progressCloud save, so you can recover progress on a new deviceGoogle Firebase Firestore
The commander name you choose (optional)Shown on public leaderboardsGoogle Firebase Firestore
Gameplay events (for example tutorial steps, battles, purchases) and device information (model, OS version, country)To understand how the Game is played and improve itGoogle Analytics for Firebase
Advertising ID (IDFA/AAID), IP address and ad interaction dataTo show rewarded video ads, measure them and prevent fraudGoogle AdMob
Purchase history and a store transaction IDTo deliver and restore in-app purchasesRevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell your personal information.

Ads and tracking

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward.

  • iOS: we ask for permission before using your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalised.
  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later in your device settings.

Buying the "No Ads" pass skips all ads.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Cloud saves and leaderboard entries are kept while you play.
  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

To delete your data, email games@bradblake.co.uk with your leaderboard commander name (or roughly when you started playing) and we will remove your cloud save and leaderboard entries within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to its processing. Contact games@bradblake.co.uk. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
