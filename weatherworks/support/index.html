Support

Help with the Weatherworks weather company game for iPhone and Android

Contact

Email games@bradblake.co.uk and we'll reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Build rooms where customers buy weather, hire staff to run them and keep everyone happy with benches, decor and a clean floor. The purple guide walks you through your first day, and the bar at the bottom of the screen always tells you what needs doing next. Hit every goal in Goals to unlock the next town.

I bought something and it's missing

Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account. Tap Goals > Restore purchases on the same account to get Weatherworks Plus back. If something still isn't there, email us with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history.

Ads and privacy

You can change your advert choices in the game under Goals > Ad privacy. Read the full privacy policy.
