How do I play?

Settle the moor, gather food, wood, gold and stone, raise an army and burn the rival Great Hall before they burn yours. Level 1 has a short tutorial, and the goal box at the top of the screen always tells you what to do next. Win levels to earn stars and Crowns, then spend Crowns on Kingdom upgrades.

What is Rival Lords?

From level 3 you can raid other players' towns. Your warband has five minutes: destroy half the town, the Great Hall, then everything, for up to three stars. Each time you win a battle, your own town goes online for others to raid while you're away.

I bought something and it's missing

Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account. Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases on the same account to get the Founder's Pack back. Crown packs are added as soon as the store confirms them. If something still isn't there, email us with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history.

Deleting your Rival Lords data

Email us with your lord name (shown on the Raids screen) and we'll delete your town and scores within 30 days.
