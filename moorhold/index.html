Moorhold

Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026 · Applies to the Moorhold game for iPhone and Android

In short
  • You don't need an account. Your campaign, Crowns and settings stay on your phone unless you turn on cloud save.
  • The game uses a random player ID to run online features: Rival Lords raids, leaderboards, the Weekly Challenge and anonymous gameplay statistics.
  • Cloud save is optional. It uses Sign in with Apple, and we never receive your name or email address.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored about you from inside the game.
  • Adverts only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. They come from Google AdMob. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Moorhold is made by Bradley Blake, based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at games@bradblake.co.uk.

What the game stores on your device

Your campaign progress, stars, Crowns, Kingdom upgrades, saved battle and settings are saved on your phone. Deleting the app removes them, unless you have turned on cloud save.

Your random player ID

The first time the game goes online, it signs in to our game server without asking for any personal details and is given a random player ID. This ID isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple account. The online features below are tied to it.

Rival Lords (online raids)

When you open Rival Lords, or win a battle after unlocking it, the game signs in to our game server without asking for any personal details. The server stores:

  • a random player ID created by the game. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple or Google account.
  • the lord name the game generated for you (for example "Sir Aldric of Blackmoor")
  • the layout and defenders of your town, taken after your latest victory
  • your Glory score and the results of raids you make or receive

Other players can see your lord name, Glory score and town. The game uses this data only to run raids and the leaderboard.

Leaderboards and the Weekly Challenge

When you finish a Last Stand or Weekly Challenge, the game sends the wave you reached, with your player ID and lord name, so it can appear on the in-game leaderboards. Other players can see your lord name and score.

Gameplay statistics

To find out where players get stuck and make the game better, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to our server, linked only to your random player ID. Examples: a level started, won or abandoned; how far the tutorial got; which shop item was tapped; the device model; and the average frame rate. These events contain no name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID. They are kept for up to 18 months and then deleted.

Cloud save (optional)

If you choose to turn on cloud save, you sign in with Apple. Apple gives us only a private, app-specific user identifier. The game doesn't ask Apple for your name or email address, so we never receive them. Your save (progress, Crowns, upgrades, purchases and settings) is stored on our server under that identifier, so you can restore it on another device. You can sign out at any time.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, the game can report your achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple Game Center. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Your Game Center friends and other players may see your scores. We don't receive your Game Center details.

Reminders

If you switch reminders on, the game schedules notifications on your phone (for example, when your daily battle is ready). These are created on your device. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us. The server is run for us by Supabase, hosted in Europe. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Advertising

Moorhold shows video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward, such as free Crowns or a free Royal Guard. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. On iPhone you can allow or block tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads. The Founder's Pack replaces all adverts with instant rewards.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as Crown packs and the Founder's Pack, are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought. We never see your card or billing details.

Legal basis

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR we rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest for running online features, anonymous gameplay statistics, non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the game working. Cloud save runs because you ask for it.

Keeping and deleting data

Online data is kept while you play. Gameplay statistics are deleted after 18 months.

To delete everything we hold about you, open the game's menu, tap Cloud save, then Delete my account. This immediately removes your player ID, town, raid history, scores, statistics and cloud save from our server. You can also email games@bradblake.co.uk with your lord name and we will remove it within 30 days. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also stop using it for Moorhold in your iPhone's Settings > [your name] > Sign in with Apple.

Children

Moorhold is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how it is used. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
