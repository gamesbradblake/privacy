Snug Life

Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Applies to the Snug Life game for iPhone and Android

In short
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up. Your home, flats, tenants, coins and settings are saved on your phone.
  • If you use the Neighbourhood (visiting other players' streets), the game stores a random player ID, the street name you picked from our list, your level and your street's layout on our game server, so other players can visit it.
  • Adverts only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. They come from Google AdMob. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Snug Life is made by Bradley Blake, based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at games@bradblake.co.uk.

What the game stores on your device

Your characters, home, flats, furniture, tenants, coins, gems, progress and settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them. Your character's name stays on your phone and is never shared.

The Neighbourhood (online play)

When you open the Neighbourhood, the game signs in to our game server without asking for any personal details. The server stores:

  • a random player ID created by the game. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple or Google account.
  • the street name you chose from our list of names (for example "Honey Lane")
  • your level and a copy of your street: furniture, floors, wall colours, and the appearance of your character and tenants
  • hearts you give and receive, gifts you send (with your street name), and any reports you make about another street

Other players can see your street name, level, hearts and the layout of your street. This data is used only to run the Neighbourhood, gifts and the weekly leaderboard. The server is Google Firebase, hosted in Europe. See Google's privacy policy.

Anonymous play statistics

To improve the game, Snug Life sends a small daily summary to our game server, linked only to the random player ID described above. It includes your level, how long you played that day, how many items, flats, tenants and pets you have, which features you used (such as photo mode or mini-games), and how many ads you watched or purchases you made. It contains no name, contact details or location, and it is never used for advertising.

Reminders and photos

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example when rent is ready) are scheduled on your phone itself, so nothing is sent to us. Photos you take in photo mode stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

Advertising

Snug Life shows video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward, such as doubling your rent or free gems. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. On iPhone you can allow or block tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as gem packs, the Season Pass and the Snug Club subscription, are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment details. Purchases are confirmed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous ID and the store's receipt. We never see your card or billing details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Moderation

Street names come from a fixed list, so players can't write messages to each other. Every street has a Report button. Reported streets are hidden from the person who reported them, and we review reports and remove streets that break the rules.

Legal basis

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR we rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest for running the Neighbourhood, non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the game working.

Keeping and deleting data

Neighbourhood data is kept while you play. To have it deleted, email games@bradblake.co.uk with your street name (shown in the Neighbourhood under My street) and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

Snug Life is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how it is used. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
