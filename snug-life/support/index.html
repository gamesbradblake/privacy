Help with Snug Life, the cosy life and landlord game for iPhone and Android

Contact

Email games@bradblake.co.uk and we'll reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Look after your character's needs, decorate your home, and turn the run-down terrace into the cosiest street in town. Renovate flats, choose tenants, grant their wishes and collect rent. The short tutorial covers the basics, and the flag button shows today's jobs, life goals and the season.

What is the Neighbourhood?

Tap the globe button to name your street, then visit other players' streets. Give hearts to the streets you love and send gifts. The streets with the most hearts each week (Monday to Sunday) win prizes, which you claim from My street.

My tenant is unhappy or complaining

Tap them to see what they need. Every flat needs a bed, a fridge or cooker, and a shower or bath, all reachable with a free tile in front. Broken items show a red bubble. Tap them to repair.

I bought something and it's missing

Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account. Gem packs are added as soon as the store confirms them. If something still isn't there, email us with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history.

Reporting a street

While visiting, tap Report at the bottom of the screen. You won't see that street again, and we review every report.

Deleting your Neighbourhood data

Email us with your street name (shown under My street) and we'll delete your street and scores within 30 days.
