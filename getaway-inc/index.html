Getaway Inc.

Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Applies to the Getaway Inc. game for iPhone and Android

In short
  • There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Adverts only play when you choose to watch one for a bonus. They are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device information to show and measure ads. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat confirming them. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Getaway Inc. is made by Bradley Blake, based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at games@bradblake.co.uk.

What the game stores on your device

Your company, staff, money, research, progress, settings and play statistics are saved on your phone only. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes them. If you choose to tap Send feedback in the game, the report it shows you (your answers and play statistics, with no personal details) is sent only to whoever you share it with.

Advertising

Getaway Inc. shows video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward, such as doubling a day's takings. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Goals > Ad privacy. On iPhone, you can also allow or block tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as Getaway Plus, are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase provider, receives a random app user ID and the store's purchase receipt so the game can unlock and restore what you bought. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your card or billing details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Legal basis

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR we rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in funding a free game for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the game working.

Children

Getaway Inc. is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how it is used. Because progress is stored only on your device, we usually hold no data about you ourselves. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
